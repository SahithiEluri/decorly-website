GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Can Decorly be customized for our decoration business?

Personalization is part of our proposed vision. We hope to support a co-branded workspace displaying Decorly alongside your business logo and configurable workflows or preferences. Exact customization would depend on your requirements and what is feasible in the product; nothing is available or guaranteed yet. Please tell us what you need in the feedback form.

Can we use Decorly without AI, or add AI later?

That is our intended direction: core management features without requiring AI, with optional AI assistance that could be added later. No plans, rates or upgrade terms have been finalized, and early testing access has not been announced.

Is Decorly available to use today?

Not yet. Decorly is in development and we are gathering feedback to decide which features matter most.

Is the AI Design Studio required?

No. AI-assisted concepts are an optional feature we are exploring. Decorators can continue to use their own designs and inspiration.

What kinds of events is Decorly intended for?

Weddings, multi-function celebrations, ceremonies, parties and other professionally decorated events.

What happens when I share feedback?

You will be directed to a Google Form where you can tell us about your work, challenges and feature preferences. Participation does not guarantee pilot access.

Can I try Decorly now?

Not yet. This website describes a proposed product. If you are interested in future prototype testing, you can leave contact details and opt in using the feedback form.

How will my contact details be used?

We use survey responses to understand decorators’ needs. Contact details are requested for optional research follow-up and future testing invitations only if you agree. Do not submit confidential client information.

Does AI replace the decorator?

No. AI assistance is optional and planned. Decorators would review designs, feasibility, material availability and prices before sharing anything with a client.
